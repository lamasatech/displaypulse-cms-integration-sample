# Support

## Questions and bugs about this sample

Open an issue in this repository and choose **Bug in the sample** or **Question about the sample**.
Issues are public: never include your site token, logs that contain a token, device names or
customer data.

## Your DisplayPulse account, devices, plans or data

Don't use public issues for these. Contact your LamasaTech account manager, or use the
[contact form](https://displaypulse.com/contact/).
